ZAL Port (Barcelona) vs Barcelona-El Prat airport easements

How the RD 657/2022 aeronautical easements (strictest of the aerodrome, radio-electric and operational surfaces) limit building heights in the ZAL Port logistics area, as built and with every building +20 m. Built with free and open data and tools.

Study area and data

The ZAL Port target area is OpenStreetMap way 1554492934, "Zona d'Activitats Logístiques del Port" (60.2 ha, Barcelona municipality). It is bounded by Carrer A and the ZAL Service Center (W), Av. dels Ports d'Europa (S), Ronda del Port / Far del Llobregat (E) and the rail corridor (N).

Data sources: AESA KMZ of RD 657/2022, ICGC/AMB LiDAR 2022 (25 cm DSM/DTM), DG Catastro INSPIRE buildings, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Heights are m AMSL (Alicante).

Geometric prototype only. The AESA KMZ has no legal value; RD 657/2022 and AESA's prior agreement (RD 369/2023) prevail. Building heights come from 2022 LiDAR and include rooftop elements.
